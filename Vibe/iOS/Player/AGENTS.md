# The now-playing card (iOS)

The scrubber itself is `WaveformUI/iOS/AGENTS.md`; the shell is `../AGENTS.md`.

## PlayerViewController

The track pager and the chrome over it. It observes `PlaybackController` and owns no playback state. **Only the settled page commits** (`commitVisiblePage`, `+Pager`); pages appearing switch nothing. Asynchronous results land in `+Delivery`, metadata excepted — that is the model's `PlaybackObserver` event.

**`presented` gates a keep-alive card.** Minimized, the card still lays out and reloads, so `commitVisiblePage` must not run — a playlist replacement settles a scroll nobody made — and neither may the playhead display link. The link reads the exact `sceneActive` the scene delegate supplies, never process-wide notifications.

**The swipe down is arbitrated on axis in `gestureRecognizerShouldBegin:`.** A paging scroll view's pan begins on movement in any direction, so the pager's pan requires the minimize pan to fail, and the minimize pan fails itself on the first horizontal move. **TRAP: the axis test is on TRANSLATION, not velocity** — velocity reads zero whenever the finger pauses, including the moment a slow drag crosses the slop.

**TRAP: the pager hold's release is matched against the view that took it, not the bound page.** The hold serves a scrub, a pinch and an FX pad under a finger (`setPagerHeld:byView:`), and its holders are a set (`_pagerHoldViews`): a scrub and a pad hold overlap, and the pager is free only when the last lifts. A track ending mid-drag rebinds the chrome to the next page while the finger is down on the outgoing one; filtering the lift on the binding drops it and the pager stays unswipeable. Why the pager must be held still at all is `WaveformUI/iOS/AGENTS.md`.

`TrackPagerView` declines its pan for touches inside a *loaded* `WaveformScrubberView`; an unloaded scrubber disables its pan and the pager must accept that surface, or the loading strip is a swipe dead zone. **Two fingers are a waveform zoom, never a swipe**: the pager's pan is capped at one touch and requires the scrubber's pinch to fail; the pinch takes the same pager hold a scrub does. The scrubber's own pan stays uncapped (`WaveformUI/iOS/AGENTS.md`).

**The right time control shows the duration; a tap flips it to minus-prefixed remaining**, held by `AppSettings.showRemainingTime`. Every render path goes through `VibeRightTimeText`. The mode is one setting, so the handler repaints every visible page. **A settings change arrives from outside the card**: `VibeDisplaySettingsDidChangeNotification` lands on `displaySettingsDidChange`, which reconfigures every visible page, syncs waveform style and repaints times; a pooled cell is configured from scratch on its way back, and `willDisplayCell:` re-applies zoom and style.

**A page that is not the current track rests at the start**: `willDisplayCell:` writes its waveform progress to 0 beside its resting times. Nothing else writes a page once the chrome leaves it, and committing a page plays from the top, so without it a played page swiped back to shows where it was left and jumps to the start as the swipe settles.

**The time labels belong to a scrub while one runs**: `+Delivery` renders `didScrubToProgress:` as the landing time, guarded to whole seconds, and `updatePlaybackUI` bails on `isScrubbing`. The duration falls back to the track's own, so scrubbing a parked track reads correctly.

**The waveform zoom is one value for the whole pager**, stored by `+Delivery`'s `didChangeVisibleFraction:` in `VibeiOSWaveformZoom` and re-applied on every `willDisplayCell:`. What is stored is the user's request, never what a view drew (`WaveformUI/iOS/AGENTS.md`).

## Output route

`OutputRouteView` rides the page, in the action bar's capsule in both layouts: the rest of portrait's bar, and a pill of its own in landscape's bottom-trailing corner. **It spans the capsule, inset from its ends**, because the view is the tap surface: hugging its content, a lone glyph left a 44pt target in the middle of a capsule that reads as one button. Landscape turns that around — the pill hugs the view, so a lone glyph at the view's 44pt minimum makes the pill the FX pad's circle. A long device name truncates inside it; landscape caps the width, and the pill gives to the transport row before it overlaps it. **On the phone's own speaker it draws the AirPlay glyph alone** — the control advertises what tapping it does; only off-device does it name the destination (`Audio/iOS/OutputRouteRules.h` decides). `AVRoutePickerView` is the only way to raise the picker, so the view fills its bounds with one, tints cleared, as an invisible tap surface under our icon and label; **its class is in `gestureRecognizer:shouldReceiveTouch:`'s decline list** because what hit-tests inside it is AVKit's view. The controller pushes one route to every visible page.

**The picker's sheet holds the playhead display link, and the hold releases itself.** **TRAP: AVKit does not reliably send the end edge** — on the simulator, never — which would freeze the waveform under correct labels for the life of the process. A generation-stamped deadline releases it, and the scene-active edge settles it sooner. The end edge also re-reads the route, because a destination picked against an inactive session posts no notification. `ui.routePickerUp` in `dump_state` shows the flag; `set_output_route` draws any route kind.

## FX pad

`FXPadView` rides the page as the route control does: a circle at the leading end of portrait's action bar, labelled FX, with the route capsule taking the rest of the width — or the whole width with Settings > Playback > Enable audio effects off, which `configurePage` applies through `setFXPadShown:` on every configure, so a recycled cell and the settings notification both land. Landscape puts the same circle in the bottom-leading corner, where the pad still grows up and to the right. **It owns its touch from the press**: a zero-duration long press that never fails on movement, so a press-and-drag in one motion works and a press-and-hold does too. The pager's pan requires it to fail (`requireGestureRecognizerToFail:`, as for the scrub pan), which alone keeps the pager off it since the press never fails while a finger is down; the card's own gestures decline it by class in `gestureRecognizer:shouldReceiveTouch:`, since they have no failure requirement. Under the finger the circle grows into a square up and to the right of its bottom-left corner, and a cursor follows the finger, clamped to the square. The square is capped by `padExtent`, which the pad measures from its superview's live safe area whenever it is asked. **TRAP: never snapshot it from the cell's `layoutSubviews`.** A recycled page lays out before its pad is placed and before it has a safe area, then settles in a content-view pass the cell never sees, so after a few track changes the stored extent read `{386, 40}` and the pad opened no bigger than its circle. **The square is glass, and only under a finger**: the effect is animated in on the press and back to nil on the lift, and the view is hidden at rest, so the live backdrop filter the page itself avoids (`Util/iOS/AGENTS.md`) runs for the length of a hold and never otherwise.

**The press point is the origin of both axes and the square's top and right edges are 1**, so a press with no movement is silent wherever it lands on the circle and there is no dead zone to tune; below or left of the press clamps to 0, and a window that caps the pad near the circle's own size still leaves a usable span (`kFXPadMinimumSpan`). The pad reports normalized positions only; what they mean is the model's — `PlaybackController.setFXPadPosition:engaged:` over `AudioFXMath.h`: y the low kill's cutoff, x the reverb's level and past the onset the 1/8-note delay's — and every position takes the same pager hold a scrub does. **Momentary**: the lift collapses the pad and releases every effect, the tails ringing out. **A track change is a lift**: the effects belong to the track they were played over, so the model cuts them on the index change (`PlaybackController`) and the card dismisses the held pad, found among the pager's holders because it need not be on the bound page. The finger still down does nothing until it lifts and presses again. **TRAP: a page recycled mid-hold releases in `prepareForReuse` (`cancelInteraction`)**, the scrubber's own trap — a track ending mid-hold scrolls the outgoing page away, and without it the effects stayed engaged and the pager locked. The debug channel's `set_fx_pad` drives the funnel without a finger; `ui.fxPadShown` and `ui.fxPadEngaged` report the view, `fx` the intent the model holds.

## TrackPageCell

One full-screen page: blurred art (a baked image, `Util/iOS/AGENTS.md`), header, transport row. A layout swapped on the cell's own bounds in `applyLayoutForBounds:`: portrait is the centered card, landscape the mac window transplanted, and a short iPad window one of the two compact layouts below. The three wide layouts share one header and differ in the body. Geometry constants are private to the cell.

**The compact layouts are the mac's transport-over-art, for a short iPad window.** A wide iPad window under `kCellCompactMaximumHeight` (600pt, a design threshold rather than a fit — the transport row fits lower, but crowds the waveform), or a taller-than-wide one under the portrait chain's 480pt floor, keeps landscape's header and hides the FX pad, the route pill and the shuffle and repeat flanks outright, so they leave hit testing and accessibility. **A mode that is on while its flank is hidden by the layout leads the codec line as a glyph** (`applyFileInfoText`), so the card never hides a mode that is on. Previous, play and next ride the art's lower edge in the mac's geometry (46pt targets at its pitch, a 31pt play glyph) over the mac's `buttonGradient`, always on, since iOS has no hover to fade it with — so the buttons always sit on dark, the mac's rule for an enabled gradient. The times move into the header's bottom corners, the mac's corner readouts, keeping the right time's tap. **Compact** gives the waveform its own full-width row off the safe bottom and lets the art take the height above it, capped at half the width so the names keep room. **The strip** is the mac window: in a window at least twice as wide as tall (`kCellStripAspect`), or too short for the waveform's row, the art fills the band and the names, waveform and times sit beside it. **iPhone never goes compact**: its landscape is fixed and keeps the full layout. The choice reads the bounds, the safe area and the layout constants, never a layout, so applying it cannot feed back. **iPadOS 26 holds every window at least about 486pt tall**, whatever `sizeRestrictions` asks, so on an iPad the strip comes by width (about 972pt and up), never by height; the height paths serve a host that allows shorter windows.

**Landscape is three rows**: the header in the top-leading corner (art on the safe area's leading edge, labels beside it, the info label top-trailing), the transport along the bottom between the FX pad and the route pill, and the waveform centered in what is left between the art's bottom and the transport's top, with the time row centered between the drawn waveform and the pills. The artist over the title centers vertically on the art, and the info label's first line tops out with the artist's, matched by cap height because the two fonts differ. The match is on the fonts the labels hold, so an artist long enough to shrink to fit drifts from it. **The artist and title draw about a third larger than portrait's** (`kCellHeaderFontScaleLandscape`), set with the layout swap, so portrait's label band still measures portrait's fonts. **Each side is one column edge**: the art and the FX pad share the leading one, the info label and the route pill the trailing one. It is the safe area's side, because the art sits at the island's height and cannot go further out; a window with no side insets (iPad) falls back to `kCellEdgeInsetLandscape`. **Each time label rides the pill under it**: the elapsed time is centered over the FX pad, and the right time is centered over the route pill's lone glyph or right-aligned with the device name when the pill draws one — which is why the route reaches a page through `TrackPageCell.setOutputRouteKind:deviceName:`, never the route view directly.

**The info label is the mac's two lines in landscape — the codec line over tempo and key — and one joined line in portrait**, whose label band reserves a single line. The cell keeps the two strings and composes per layout (`applyFileInfoText`). The key is the tagged one in Camelot: key analysis and the notation setting are macOS-only.

**The transport is always up**; only the empty state fades it, with the action bar, the route control and the FX pad (`chromeAlpha`), and only the compact layouts hide the latter three. **Only the play button pauses**: the card has no tap of its own. **Shuffle and repeat flank the row** — shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat — in narrower targets with smaller glyphs, off drawn dimmed. Settings > Appearance > Show shuffle and repeat hides both (`setShuffleRepeatShown:`, applied on every configure like the FX pad): their widths and outer gaps drop to zero, so the three get their full spacing back. Hidden, both modes are off (`Settings/AGENTS.md`), so the card never hides a mode that is on. **The cell hides them too wherever the row cannot fit them**, and in the compact layouts: shown is the setting AND the fit, which `applyShuffleRepeatShown` decides in `layoutSubviews` from the bounds, the safe area and the constants the row and both layouts are built from — never from the row's own layout, so applying the answer cannot feed back. With the flanks the row needs 334pt, which a 320pt window (Slide Over) cannot give in portrait nor landscape under about 510pt, and over it a required constraint broke every pass. The four gaps are layout guides held equal in pairs, so the three stay centered: the flanks' gaps give first, then the three's, against the screen edges in portrait and the FX pad and route pill in landscape — where the row centers between the two pills rather than on the screen, since the pill grows with a device name — all below the route view's compression resistance, so the row closes up before a device name truncates. **Next dims at the end of the playlist, from the PAGE's index**, so the last page arrives dimmed — except that the current page asks the playlist's `hasNextTrack`, since under shuffle only it knows its next, and under Repeat All no page is last. `applyPlayOrderToVisiblePages` restates Next and both flanks when the cursor or a mode moves. **Paging to the current track animates only to a neighbor**: under shuffle the next track is usually pages away, and scrolling through every page between would read as a swipe. The pager pages in row order, so a swipe is a manual pick, which shuffle splices into its order without a repeat. One trap under the dimmed looks:

- **TRAP: the disabled look is drawn, not delegated.** A system button dims its own template image, so an alpha on top compounds. `setGlyph:onButton:pointSize:` installs a pre-tinted `AlwaysOriginal` image for the disabled state.

**Portrait is four bands and only the art band moves**: grabber strip, art band (the leftover height, art centered in it), fixed label band, and the action bar, transport and waveform off the safe bottom with the time row off the waveform. **The art is capped twice**: a width fraction (the binding cap on tall screens) and the band (short screens).

**The waveform sits at the same y on every page — a layout guarantee.** The label band's height is its worst case: two-line title, one line each for artist and codec. The one exception is the codec line leaving the band with its gap when the setting is off or a track has no readout yet; that is one setting across every page, so the guarantee holds and the art moves instead. Labels shrink to fit, never truncate, and ride centered in the band with the single-line labels' lines reserved, so a missing artist lays out like a present one. The art card gives at accessibility sizes.

**TRAP: the art card's shadow path is restated from the card's OWN layout pass** (`TrackPageArtCardView`). Its size is set by the contentView's constraints, so a label-band change resizes it without the cell's `layoutSubviews` running; set from the cell, the shadow keeps the previous size until a swipe recycles the cell.

**The time row is pulled up into the waveform view's bottom** (`kCellTimeWaveformOverlap`): the scrubber reserves headroom around the envelope, and the eye measures from the drawn waveform. It hangs off the waveform so tightening it cannot push the waveform down. Landscape measures its time band from the same line.

**A page shows full-size art or the vinyl placeholder, never the 128px thumbnail.** The **art window** in `+Pager` decodes the current page and its neighbors ahead and holds their art (`_artHeldPages`) to a byte budget — **but never releases a page inside the window or with a live cell**, whose image view pins the bitmap anyway. `renderHeaderForTrack:` moves the window, and a metadata delivery re-runs it: before metadata lands, the art dispatch is a message to nil. **The commit path discards nothing**, since the departing page is usually the arriving one's neighbor.

## PageWaveformCoordinator

Waveform bookkeeping between `AudioWaveformCache`, which runs one load at a time, and the cells.

**Deliveries carry the track they were loaded for**, and a stale one is dropped on its `sourceKey` — the app-wide staleness guarantee, by window because cue rows of one file each have a waveform; `requestIndex:track:` records `_targetKey` beside `_targetIndex`. A page already targeted is left alone, since re-requesting per reload would keep killing the decode. A matching terminal failure clears the target and lets the next request retry. **A buffering hold leaves the waveform drawn and scrubbable**: `didChangeBuffering:forTrack:` sends only `playbackDidFinishLoading:` when it ends, and Now Playing publishes rate 0 while it lasts; the mini player has no loading indicator. **A provider's dataless file is not asked for** (`requestCurrentWaveform`, `isDatalessFile:`), since its decode would hold a slot through its download; the card asks again when the open settles (`playbackDidFinishLoading:`). **A Dropbox placeholder is asked for**: its stat is the file's, so the cache answers for an evicted track parked at relaunch, and a miss is refused at the open at once — not a failure to draw (`didFailWaveformForIndex:` keeps the indicator), since the download will land and the open asks again. **A placeholder with a live stream decodes as it downloads**: its handle rides the transfer the play already holds and starts none (`Audio/Waveform/AGENTS.md`), so the page fills in as the bytes arrive, while a page swiped past still downloads nothing. The snapshot window prunes to a radius around the current page. The BPM delivery is forwarded as delivered, matched by track rather than page and never held — the model stamps it (`PlaybackController.noteDetectedBPM:forTrack:`) and the page hears it back as the metadata event, **only when the tempo the row shows changed**: the delivery repeats on every load of a file, cache hits included, and unguarded it repainted a page per track change and under a swipe. **The card clears the waveform cache once** (`kWaveformTempoBackfillKey`), because an entry cached before tempo detection reached iOS carries no BPM and a cache hit never re-analyzes; the key delivery is not, since key analysis is macOS-only.

**The scroll hold (`held`) is the pager's frame budget.** A delivery re-bakes a scrubber, and a request cancels the cache's one load — so a swipe across N pages cancelled N decodes and finished none. Held, deliveries are recorded but not forwarded and requests are dropped. `+Pager` holds for a user swipe, a visible programmatic page animation and a size transition (a minimized page move snaps without one), and clears the swipe hold **before** `commitVisiblePage` so the settled page's request gets through. **Every programmatic retarget renews a generation-stamped deadline**, since UIKit may never deliver its end callback; the deadline releases the hold and reissues the current page's request. The same hold pauses the playhead display link (`updateScrollLinkState`).

**Cached neighbors are prepared before a swipe.** The cache-only reads of the adjacent pages (`fetchNeighborWaveforms`) are issued when the cursor moves, after the current page's request, and again when the frame-budget hold releases, since a held coordinator drops them; the refresh only bakes. The current page's request goes first, so its disk read is ahead of the neighbors' on the cache's serial queue — an ordering, not a guarantee: a neighbor's stat on the preview lane can still finish before a slow stat of the current track. Misses start no decode and leave the current load alone. Replies match the retained request token, so a cancelled read cannot land in a later request for the same track. Replacement, pruning and reset cancel pending reads; a running stat retains its bounded slot until it returns. Nothing remembers a miss, so the next cursor move asks again; a read pending for the same track is not repeated. The coordinator keeps snapshots two pages out against a fetch radius of one on purpose: the departed page's snapshot is kept for the swipe back. The art window's radius of two is the art decode's lead, a separate concern. Complete neighbors are rendered in hidden scrubbers inheriting the card's traits, at the current scrubber's geometry and zoom. The window retains one prepared scrubber per neighbor, plus an incoming current page until its live cell shows the snapshot (`hydrateWaveformInCell:` or the next refresh releases it). A cursor move alone cannot release that image: Next renders the new header before its cell appears. Pages outside the window, playlist replacements and minimizing release their preparations. A live neighbor donates its existing pixels rather than rendering them again. Artwork completion, settings, zoom, a changed layout size and complete waveform deliveries refresh the same window; partial deliveries leave neighbors alone. Pages and preparations read the same displayed art, including the placeholder, for their palette. Neither the refresh nor the neighbor fetch performs a filesystem probe: the cache-only reader computes its key on a separate bounded preview lane, and never opens a dataless file. `hydrateWaveformInCell:` copies matching prepared images into the arriving cell before it is displayed, without an entrance animation; a stale geometry or palette falls back to rendering the cached snapshot without animation. The playback open hydrates the destination instead of clearing it; a slow open overlays the loading indicator until settlement, even without transfer progress. The slow-open state is the model's `currentOpenSlow` (cleared by every cursor change, every replacement and every play, set before `playbackDidBeginLoading:` is sent); every edge re-applies it to the visible pages (`applyPlaybackLoadingToVisiblePages`) and `willDisplayCell:` to each page as it appears, so a page off screen at an edge cannot keep a stale shimmer or miss one. A track change cancels held scrubs and pinches explicitly, preserving the pixels and discarding the pending seek. Fresh waveform deliveries keep their entrance animation, and actual transfer progress can still draw over a cached waveform.

**Only the current track's waveform reaches the widget.** `PlaybackController.offerWaveformToWidget:forTrack:` drops any other track's, so a neighbor delivery cannot replace the current track's pending offer before publication. **TRAP: a complete cached page delivers nothing on request**, so `requestCurrentWaveform` must still offer its snapshot.
