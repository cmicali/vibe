# The now-playing card (iOS)

This directory holds the now-playing card: `PlayerViewController`, its track pager, and the pages (`TrackPageCell`). It also holds each page's FX pad and output-route indicator. `PageWaveformCoordinator` hands each page its waveform.

The scrubber itself is `WaveformUI/iOS/AGENTS.md`. The shell is `../AGENTS.md`.

## PlayerViewController

The track pager and the chrome over it. It observes `PlaybackController` and owns no playback state. **Only the settled page commits** (`commitVisiblePage`, `+Pager`). Pages appearing switch nothing. Asynchronous results land in `+Delivery`, except metadata, which is the model's `PlaybackObserver` event.

**`presented` gates a keep-alive card.** Minimized, the card still lays out and reloads. So `commitVisiblePage` must not run, or a playlist replacement settles a scroll nobody made, and neither may the playhead display link. The link reads the `sceneActive` the scene delegate supplies, never process-wide notifications.

**The swipe down is arbitrated on axis in `gestureRecognizerShouldBegin:`.** A paging scroll view's pan begins on movement in any direction. So the pager's pan requires the minimize pan to fail, and the minimize pan fails itself on the first horizontal move. **TRAP: the axis test is on translation, not velocity.** Velocity reads zero whenever the finger pauses, including the moment a slow drag crosses the slop.

**`TrackPagerView` declines its pan for touches inside a loaded `WaveformScrubberView`.** TRAP: requiring the scrubber's pan to fail is not enough. At a content edge UIKit never begins the scrubber's pan, and the pager turns the page. An unloaded scrubber disables its pan, and the pager must accept that surface, or the loading strip is a swipe dead zone. **Two fingers are a waveform zoom, never a swipe**: the pager's pan is capped at one touch and requires the scrubber's pinch to fail.

**TRAP: the pager hold's release is matched against the view that took it, not the bound page.** A scrub, a pinch and an FX pad under a finger each take the hold (`setPagerHeld:byView:`). Its holders are a set (`_pagerHoldViews`), and the pager is free only when the last lifts. A track ending mid-drag rebinds the chrome to the next page while the finger is still on the outgoing one. Filtering the lift on the binding drops it, and the pager stays unswipeable. Why the pager must be held still is `WaveformUI/iOS/AGENTS.md`.

**TRAP: dequeue is not the last word** (`willDisplayCell:`). A prefetched page is configured off screen and misses deliveries, since `refreshPageAtIndex:` reaches only live cells. So `willDisplayCell:` configures the page again, re-applies zoom and style, and the slow-open shimmer. **A page that is not the current track rests at the start**: `willDisplayCell:` writes its waveform progress to 0 beside its resting times. Nothing else writes a page once the chrome leaves it.

**The right time shows the duration. A tap flips it to minus-prefixed remaining**, held in `AppSettings.showRemainingTime`. Every render path goes through `VibeRightTimeText`, and the tap repaints every visible page. **A settings change arrives from outside the card**: `VibeDisplaySettingsDidChangeNotification` lands on `displaySettingsDidChange`, which reconfigures every visible page, syncs the waveform style and repaints the times.

**The time labels belong to a scrub while one runs.** `+Delivery` renders `didScrubToProgress:` as the landing time, and `updatePlaybackUI` bails on `isScrubbing`. The duration falls back to the track's own, so scrubbing a parked track reads correctly.

**The waveform zoom is one value for the whole pager.** `+Delivery`'s `didChangeVisibleFraction:` stores it in `VibeiOSWaveformZoom`, and every `willDisplayCell:` re-applies it. What is stored is the user's request, never what a view drew (`WaveformUI/iOS/AGENTS.md`).

## Output route

`OutputRouteView` rides the page in the action bar's capsule: the rest of portrait's bar, or a pill of its own in landscape's bottom-trailing corner. **TRAP: it spans the capsule, because the view is the tap surface.** Hugging its content, a lone glyph left a 44pt target in a capsule that reads as one button. Landscape turns this around: the pill hugs the view, so a lone glyph makes the pill the FX pad's circle. **TRAP: the hug sits below the label's compression resistance**, or a device name truncates with room to spare. **On the phone's own speaker it draws the AirPlay glyph alone.** Only off-device does it name the destination (`Audio/iOS/OutputRouteRules.h` decides). `AVRoutePickerView` is the only way to raise the picker, so the view fills its bounds with one, tints cleared, under our icon and label. Its class is in `gestureRecognizer:shouldReceiveTouch:`'s decline list, because what hit-tests inside it is AVKit's view. The controller pushes one route to every visible page.

**The picker's sheet holds the playhead display link, and the hold releases itself.** **TRAP: AVKit does not reliably send the end edge**, and on the simulator never does. Stuck, the waveform freezes under correct labels. A generation-stamped deadline (`kRoutePickerHoldSeconds`) releases it, and the scene-active edge settles it sooner. The end edge also re-reads the route, because a destination picked against an inactive session posts no notification. `ui.routePickerUp` in `dump_state` shows the flag, and `set_output_route` draws any route kind.

## FX pad

`FXPadView` rides the page as the route control does. In portrait it is a circle labelled FX at the leading end of the action bar, and the route capsule takes the rest. With Settings > Playback > Enable audio effects off, the route takes the whole width. `configurePage` applies that through `setFXPadShown:` on every configure, so a recycled cell and the settings notification both land. Landscape puts the circle in the bottom-leading corner.

**It owns its touch from the press**: a zero-duration long press that never fails on movement, so press-and-drag and press-and-hold both work. The pager's pan requires it to fail (`requireGestureRecognizerToFail:`). The card's own gestures decline it by class in `gestureRecognizer:shouldReceiveTouch:`. Under the finger the circle grows into a square up and to the right of its bottom-left corner, with a cursor clamped to it. The square is capped by `padExtent`, measured from the superview's live safe area when asked. **TRAP: never store it from the cell's `layoutSubviews`.** A recycled page lays out before its pad is placed and before it has a safe area. An extent captured there can limit the pad to its resting circle. **The square is glass only under a finger.** The effect animates in on the press and back to nil on the lift, and the view is hidden at rest. So the live backdrop filter the page avoids (`Util/iOS/AGENTS.md`) runs only during a hold.

**The press point is the origin of both axes.** Each axis reaches 1 at the corresponding edge or after `kFXPadMinimumSpan`, whichever is farther. A press with no movement is silent wherever it lands, so there is no dead zone to tune. Below or left of the press clamps to 0. `kFXPadMinimumSpan` keeps a usable span in a window that caps the pad near the circle's size. The pad reports normalized positions only. Their meaning is the model's: `PlaybackController.setFXPadPosition:engaged:` over `AudioFXMath.h` (y the low kill's cutoff, x the reverb's level and, past the onset, the 1/8-note delay's). Every position takes the same pager hold a scrub does.

**The pad is momentary.** The lift collapses the pad and releases every effect, and the tails ring out. **A track change is a lift.** The effects belong to the track they were played over, so the model cuts them on the index change. The card dismisses the held pad, found among the pager's holders since it need not be on the bound page. The finger still down does nothing until it presses again. **TRAP: a page recycled mid-hold releases in `prepareForReuse` (`cancelInteraction`).** A track ending mid-hold scrolls the outgoing page away, and without it the effects stayed engaged and the pager locked. `set_fx_pad` drives the funnel without a finger. `ui.fxPadShown` and `ui.fxPadEngaged` report the view, and `fx` reports the model's intent.

## TrackPageCell

One full-screen page: blurred art (a baked image, `Util/iOS/AGENTS.md`), header and transport row. `applyLayoutForBounds:` swaps the layout on the cell's own bounds: portrait is the centered card, landscape is the mac window transplanted, and a short iPad window takes one of the two compact layouts. The three wide layouts share one header and differ in the body. Geometry constants are private to the cell. The small views it is built from (the action bar, the time control and the art card) are `TrackPageViews.h`.

**The compact layouts are the mac's transport-over-art, for a short iPad window.** A wide window under `kCellCompactMaximumHeight`, or a tall one under the portrait chain's floor (`kCellPortraitMinimumHeight`), keeps landscape's header. It hides the FX pad, the route pill and the shuffle and repeat flanks outright, so they leave hit testing and accessibility. **A mode that is on while its flank is hidden leads the codec line as a glyph** (`applyFileInfoText`), so the card never hides a mode that is on. Previous, play and next ride the art's lower edge in the mac's geometry over the mac's `buttonGradient`, always on, since iOS has no hover. The times move into the header's bottom corners and keep the right time's tap. **Compact** gives the waveform its own full-width row and lets the art take the height above, capped at half the width. **The strip** is the mac window: in a window at least `kCellStripAspect` times as wide as tall, or too short for the waveform's row, the art fills the band and the names, waveform and times sit beside it. **iPhone never goes compact.** The choice reads the bounds, the safe area and the constants, never a layout, so applying it cannot feed back. The system can impose a larger minimum window height.

**Landscape is three rows.** The header is in the top-leading corner: art on the safe area's leading edge, labels beside it, and the info label top-trailing. The transport runs along the bottom between the FX pad and the route pill. The waveform is centered in the space between the art's bottom and the transport's top, with the time row between the drawn waveform and the pills. The artist and title center on the art, and the info label's first line aligns with the artist's by cap height. The artist and title draw larger than portrait's (`kCellHeaderFontScaleLandscape`), set with the layout swap, so portrait's label band still measures portrait's fonts. **Each side is one column edge**: the art and the FX pad share the leading one, the info label and the route pill the trailing one. It is the safe area's side; a window with no side insets (iPad) falls back to `kCellEdgeInsetLandscape`. **Each time label rides the pill under it.** The right time centers over the route pill's lone glyph, or right-aligns with a device name. So the route reaches a page through `TrackPageCell.setOutputRouteKind:deviceName:`, never the route view directly.

**The info label is the codec line over tempo and key in landscape, and one joined line in portrait**, whose label band reserves a single line. The cell keeps the two strings and composes per layout (`applyFileInfoText`). The key is the tagged one in Camelot, since key analysis and the notation setting are macOS-only. **During a buffering hold the current page shows Buffering… with a spinner after it**, while the player's `bufferingNoticeShown` is set, half a second into the hold (`PlaybackController.bufferingNoticeShown`, `Audio/AGENTS.md`). It takes the codec line while that line has text, and portrait's joined line then drops the tempo. Otherwise it takes the artist line, whose height the band always keeps. So a hold never grows the label band or moves the card. The spinner lies over clear room at the end of its line's text. Constraints tie it to the label (`applyBufferingSpinner`), since a label can move without the cell's own layout pass. The notice's edges (`didChangeBufferingNotice:forTrack:`) send `playbackDidRenderCurrentTrack:`. `dump_state` reports it as `ui.bufferingShown`.

**The transport is always up.** Only the empty state fades it, with the action bar, the route control and the FX pad (`chromeAlpha`). **Only the play button pauses**: the card has no tap of its own. **Shuffle and repeat flank the row** (shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat) in narrower targets with smaller glyphs, drawn dimmed when off. Settings > Appearance > Show shuffle and repeat hides both (`setShuffleRepeatShown:`, applied on every configure), and hidden, both modes are off (`Settings/AGENTS.md`). **The cell also hides them wherever the row cannot fit them**, and in the compact layouts. `applyShuffleRepeatShown` decides the fit in `layoutSubviews` from the bounds, the safe area and the constants, never from the row's own layout. With the flanks the row needs 334pt, which a 320pt Slide Over window cannot give. **TRAP: the fit reads the cell's own safe area before the superclass lays out its content view.** The content view can still carry the previous orientation's insets on that pass, which left both buttons hidden after a return to portrait. The four gaps are layout guides held equal in pairs, so the three main buttons stay centered. The flanks' gaps give first, then the three's, all below the route view's compression resistance, so the row closes up before a device name truncates. In landscape the row centers between the two pills, not on the screen.

**Next dims at the end of the playlist, from the page's index**, so the last page arrives dimmed. The current page asks the playlist's `hasNextTrack` instead, since under shuffle only it knows its next, and under Repeat All no page is last. `applyPlayOrderToVisiblePages` restates Next and both flanks when the cursor or a mode moves. **Paging to the current track animates only to a neighbor**, since under shuffle the next track is usually pages away. The pager pages in row order, so a swipe is a manual pick, which shuffle splices into its order.

**TRAP: the disabled look is drawn, not delegated.** A system button dims its own template image, so an alpha on top compounds. `setGlyph:onButton:pointSize:` installs a pre-tinted `AlwaysOriginal` image for the disabled state.

**Portrait is four bands, and only the art band moves**: grabber strip, art band (the leftover height), fixed label band, then the action bar, transport and waveform off the safe bottom. **The art is capped twice**: by a width fraction (binding on tall screens) and by the band (on short screens).

**The waveform sits at the same y on every page.** The label band's height is its worst case: a two-line title and one line each for artist and codec. The label band drops the codec line when disabled or when the page has no readout. The waveform stays anchored to the safe bottom. Labels shrink to fit, never truncate, and a missing artist lays out like a present one. The art card gives at accessibility sizes.

**TRAP: the art card's shadow path is restated from the card's own layout pass** (`TrackPageArtCardView`). The contentView's constraints size the card, so a label-band change resizes it without the cell's `layoutSubviews` running. Set from the cell, the shadow keeps the previous size until a swipe recycles the cell.

**The time row is pulled up into the waveform view's bottom** (`kCellTimeWaveformOverlap`), since the scrubber reserves headroom around the envelope and the eye measures from the drawn waveform. It hangs off the waveform, so tightening it cannot push the waveform down.

**A page shows full-size art or the vinyl placeholder, never the 128px thumbnail.** The art window in `+Pager` decodes the current page and its neighbors ahead, and holds their art (`_artHeldPages`) to a byte budget. It never releases a page inside the window or with a live cell, whose image view pins the bitmap anyway. `renderHeaderForTrack:` moves the window, and a metadata delivery re-runs it, since before metadata lands the art request goes to nil. The commit path discards nothing, since the departing page is usually the arriving one's neighbor.

## PageWaveformCoordinator

Waveform bookkeeping between `AudioWaveformCache` and the cells. The cache has one delivery target. Detached decodes can still finish in the background.

**Deliveries carry the track they were loaded for, and a stale one is dropped on its `sourceKey`**, the root staleness guarantee. `requestIndex:track:` records `_targetKey` beside `_targetIndex`. A page already targeted is left alone, since re-requesting per reload would keep cancelling the decode. A matching terminal failure clears the target, so the next request retries.

- **A buffering hold leaves the waveform drawn and scrubbable.** It never sends `playbackDidBeginLoading:`. Its end sends `playbackDidFinishLoading:`. Now Playing publishes rate 0 while it lasts.
- **A provider's dataless file is not asked for** (`requestCurrentWaveform`, `isDatalessFile:`), since its decode would hold the slot through the download. The card asks again when the open settles (`playbackDidFinishLoading:`). **The dataless probe runs off main**, since it is a stat (`WaveformWaitsForOpen`). Its answer acts on main only while the page and its track's `sourceKey` still match. A neighbor's indicator waits for the same answer (`hydrateWaveformInCell:`).
- **A Dropbox placeholder is asked for.** Its stat is the file's, so the cache answers for an evicted track parked at relaunch. A miss is refused at the open at once, and `didFailWaveformForIndex:` keeps the indicator, since the download will land and the open asks again. A placeholder with a live stream decodes as it downloads, riding the play's transfer (`Audio/Waveform/AGENTS.md`).
- **The BPM delivery is forwarded as delivered**, matched by track and never held. The model stamps it (`PlaybackController.noteDetectedBPM:forTrack:`), and the page hears it back as the metadata event only when the row's tempo changed. The delivery repeats on every load, cache hits included. **The card clears the waveform cache once** under `kWaveformTempoBackfillKey`. Cache lookups also require the stored analysis flags to cover the request.

**The scroll hold (`held`) is the pager's frame budget.** A delivery re-bakes a scrubber, and a request cancels the cache's one load, so a swipe across N pages would cancel N decodes and finish none. Held, deliveries are recorded but not forwarded, and requests are dropped. `+Pager` holds for a user swipe, a visible programmatic page animation and a size transition. It clears the swipe hold **before** `commitVisiblePage`, so the settled page's request gets through. **TRAP: a programmatic scroll has no guaranteed end callback**, since `scrollViewDidEndScrollingAnimation:` never arrives for a superseded animation. Every programmatic retarget renews a generation-stamped deadline, which releases the hold and reissues the current page's request. The same hold pauses the playhead display link (`updateScrollLinkState`).

**Cached neighbors are prepared before a swipe.** `fetchNeighborWaveforms` issues cache-only reads of the adjacent pages: when the cursor moves, when the hold releases, and after an append. The current page's request waits for its dataless probe. A neighbor's read can therefore start first, on a lane of its own. Misses start no decode, and nothing remembers a miss. Replies match the retained request token, so a cancelled read cannot land in a later request for the same track. Replacement, pruning and reset cancel pending reads. Cache keys require file attributes. The cache-only reader computes them on a separate bounded preview lane and starts no decode.

**Snapshots are kept two pages out, against a fetch radius of one**, so the departed page's snapshot survives for the swipe back. Complete neighbors are baked in hidden scrubbers at the current scrubber's geometry and zoom. The pixels live in the scrubber's bake store (`WaveformUI/iOS/AGENTS.md`), not in the bakers, so releasing a baker releases no pixels. A page whose cell is up gets no baker, because the cell bakes into the store itself. `hydrateWaveformInCell:` lays the arriving cell out, since the store's key is the view's geometry, then hands it its snapshot unanimated. A zoom change re-bakes only the page under the fingers. Partial deliveries leave neighbors alone.

**The slow-open state is the model's `currentOpenSlow`.** Every edge re-applies it to the visible pages (`applyPlaybackLoadingToVisiblePages`), and `willDisplayCell:` to each arriving page, so no page keeps a stale shimmer. The playback open hydrates the destination rather than clearing it. A track change cancels held scrubs and pinches, keeping the pixels and dropping the pending seek.

**Only the current track's waveform reaches the widget.** `PlaybackController.offerWaveformToWidget:forTrack:` drops any other track's. **TRAP: a complete cached page delivers nothing on request**, and a track change clears the widget's strip, so `requestCurrentWaveform` must still offer its snapshot.
