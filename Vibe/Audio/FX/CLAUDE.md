# DJ FX (the master-bus segment)

`AudioPlayer` owns one `AudioFX` (readonly `fx`): the Q low-kill high-pass with its W double-cutoff boost, and the send-returns, E a long reverb wash, R and T BPM-synced ping-pong delays on 1/8- and 1/16-note taps fed by the controller through `delayTapBPM`. The topology and threading are `AudioFX.h`'s preamble; the AVFAudio traps (MatrixReverb's stale header ranges, dropped pre-attach mixer writes, EQ bypass clicks, and why the install guards are the nodes rather than an engine handle) are `TRAP:` comments in `AudioFX.m`.

**The whole segment is optional, decided once per player lifetime by `enableFX:` at init.** Without it `fx` is nil, no FX node is ever created or attached, and `installMasterBusOnQueue` wires the mixer straight to the output at the mixer’s own default format, also used by the iOS media-services rebuild. Only enabled macOS bit-perfect preparation changes the connection to the device rate, saving the original format for restoration when the mode is left. macOS passes `AppSettings.audioFXAllowed` at launch; iOS passes a hard `NO`. The menu and Q/W/E/R/T read `AppSettings.audioFXAllowed` live (the FX setting with bit-perfect output outranking it), so turning it off clears every active effect and withdraws the controls at once (sends close while their tails decay normally) while graph removal waits for relaunch; a run launched without the segment cannot expose the controls when the setting is turned on. The level tap's node depends on whether the segment exists (`Levels/CLAUDE.md`).

**`AudioFX` holds plain on-off state per effect; telling a tap from a hold is `TransportKeyMonitor`'s job.** One coupling is enforced here so every path gets it: **clearing `lowKillEnabled` also clears `lowKillBoostActive`**, because the boost modifies that filter and a latched W must not hold the cutoff up while the low kill reads off.

**The numbers are `AudioFXMath.h`, header-only and tested**: the three-way low-kill cutoff, the ping-pong tap and lane times with their no-tempo fallback, the lane feedback and the swell target. The fast logic suite checks the math; `VibeAudioTests` renders the real graph offline (`Tests/CLAUDE.md`).

**`installInEngine:` wires the segment once, on the player queue, during the async init; the object exists before the engine does, so intent set early is applied at install.** Every toggle is click-free (cutoff sweeps and gate fades on the player queue with generation preemption), and **off is colorless**: the low kill sweeps to the 20 Hz floor and then swaps its bands to a 0 dB parametric type, an identity biquad (round-off bounded at −200 dBFS by the render suite), because a high-pass parked at the floor still lifted the sub-bass through its resonant peak.
