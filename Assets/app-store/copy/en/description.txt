Vibe is a fast, minimal player for your music files on the Mac. No third-party engine, no account, no subscription. Play the music you already have, wherever you keep it.

Waveform
Vibe draws every track as a full waveform across the window. Click anywhere on it to seek. Vibe has no library to import and no database to rebuild. Drop a file or a folder on the window and it plays, whether it sits on your disk or in iCloud Drive or Dropbox. Vibe reads tags, artwork, and BPM in the background and caches them, so the next open is instant.

Formats
MP3, MP2, AAC, FLAC, MP4/M4A, AIFF, and WAV, all decoded by macOS itself. Lossless files play at full resolution, and each track's format and sample rate sit next to the title.

DJs Will Feel At Home
• A pitch fader laid out like a turntable's, hidden until you want it.
• Skips measured in bars, not seconds. When the tempo is known, jump 8, 16, or 32 bars and stay on the grid; when it isn't, the same keys move 10, 30, or 60 seconds.
• Performance FX under one hand: a low-kill filter, a reverb wash, and delays locked to the track's BPM. Tap a key to latch an effect; hold it for momentary.
