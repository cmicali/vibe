Vibe is a fast, minimal player for your music files. No third-party engine, no account, no subscription. Play the music you already have, wherever you keep it.

Waveform view
Vibe draws every track as a full waveform across the window. Click anywhere on it to seek.

Fast
Vibe is built to be small and fast. Tags, artwork, and waveforms are read in the background and cached, so the next open is instant.

Decoded by macOS
MP3, AAC, MP4/M4A, MP2, AIFF, WAV, and FLAC, all played through the system APIs. Lossless files play at full resolution, and each track's format and sample rate sit next to the title.

No library to import
Drop a file or a folder — from your disk, iCloud Drive, or Dropbox — and it plays. There is no database to rebuild and no library to manage. Artwork, tags, and BPM fill in behind you while the first track is already playing.

Built with DJs in mind
Vibe includes turntable-style pitch control, BPM and key analysis, bar-accurate skips, and performance FX.

Free and open source
Vibe collects nothing and shows no ads. The full source is on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license.
