First release of Vibe for iPhone and iPad.

* Open a folder from Files or the built-in file picker - on your device, in iCloud Drive, or in Dropbox.
* Full-waveform scrubbing, swipe to switch tracks
* Small and medium home-screen widgets, with waveform
* Favorites and search across the folders you have opened
* Background playback with lock screen and AirPlay controls
* MP3, AAC, M4A, MP2, AIFF, WAV, and FLAC at full resolution
