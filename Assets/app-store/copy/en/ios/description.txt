Vibe is a fast, minimal player for your music files.

Waveform view
Vibe draws every track as a full waveform. Drag it to scrub, swipe sideways to move between tracks.

Just plays your files
Play files on your iPhone, in iCloud Drive, or in Dropbox from the Files app or the built-in file picker.

No library to import
There is no database to rebuild and nothing to sync.

Decoded by iOS
MP3, AAC, MP4/M4A, MP2, AIFF, WAV, and FLAC, all played through the system APIs. Lossless files play at full resolution.

Widgets
Small and medium widgets show the current track with its artwork and seekable waveform.

Free and open source
Vibe collects nothing and shows no ads. The full source is on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license.
