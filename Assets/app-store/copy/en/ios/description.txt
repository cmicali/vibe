Vibe is a fast, minimal player for your music files.

Waveform view
Vibe draws every track as a full waveform. Drag it to scrub, and swipe sideways to move between tracks.

Your files, wherever they are
Open a folder from the Files app or the built-in file picker - on your iPhone, in iCloud Drive, or in Dropbox.

No library to import
There is no database to rebuild and nothing to sync. Artwork and tags fill in behind you while the first track is already playing, so the next open is instant.

Decoded by iOS
MP3, AAC, MP4/M4A, MP2, AIFF, WAV, and FLAC, all played through the system APIs. Lossless files play at full resolution.

Widgets
Small and medium widgets show the current track with its artwork and waveform.

Free and open source
Vibe collects nothing and shows no ads. The full source is on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license.
