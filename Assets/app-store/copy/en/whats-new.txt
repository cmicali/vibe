Third beta of Vibe 1.12 for macOS.

Changes since beta 2:

- Bit-perfect output and audio effects can now be switched on or off without restarting the app. The current track, position, and play/pause state are kept across the switch.
- Bit-perfect and exclusive output are now remembered per audio device: a device keeps its modes while unplugged and gets them back when chosen again.
- The theme editor gains a Show number column switch for the playlist.
- The waveform style picker now sits above its preview in Settings > Appearance.
- The Help menu's support item is now Report Issue or Feature Request.

Vibe 1.12 also adds Wiggle waveforms, bit-perfect playback with optional exclusive output, an audio output device picker, M3U playlist export, reopening the last playlist, and expanded theme customization. It fixes audio coloration when the low-cut effect is disabled.

Downloads:

- Universal: Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- arm64: Apple silicon Macs only.
- Use the DMG to drag Vibe into Applications. ZIP files are available as an alternative.

Both app builds and their disk images are Developer ID signed, notarized, and stapled. Requires macOS 13 or later.
