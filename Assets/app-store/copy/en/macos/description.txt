Vibe is a fast, minimal player for your music files.

Waveform view
Vibe draws every track as a full waveform across the window. Click anywhere on it to seek.

Fast
Vibe is built to be small and fast. Tags, artwork, and waveforms are read in the background and cached so the next open is instant.

Just plays your files
Drop a file or a folder - from your disk, iCloud Drive, or Dropbox - and it plays. There is no database to rebuild and no library to manage.

Decoded by macOS
MP3, AAC, MP4/M4A, MP2, AIFF, WAV, and FLAC, all played through the system APIs. Lossless files play at full resolution.

Bit-perfect mode
Bit-perfect and exclusive mode send audio to your output device exactly as-is, for the best possible audio quality.

Built with DJs in mind
Vibe includes turntable-style pitch control, BPM and key analysis, bar-accurate skips, and performance FX.

Free and open source
Vibe collects nothing and shows no ads. The full source is on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license.
