* New: a rebuilt playback engine that plays every track straight to your audio device - faster, more stable, and better at handling devices coming and going
* New: optional volume control (Settings > Audio Output), themable
* New: Lock Window Position, in the View menu and Settings > General
* New: Vibe remembers the output device you chose when it's switched off or unplugged, and switches back to it, with its Bit-perfect and Exclusive settings, when it returns
* New: a USB audio interface plugged into a different port keeps its Bit-perfect and Exclusive settings
* Changed: higher-quality MP3 decoding and sample-rate conversion, using less CPU
* Changed: when another app takes exclusive use of your audio device, Vibe pauses and says so
* Changed: losing your audio output mid-track now pauses instead of skipping ahead
* Improved: play, pause, and skip no longer wait while an audio device switches or wakes up
* Improved: a stuck network share no longer holds up other tracks, or the tags and artwork of cloud files
* Improved: quitting is instant; Vibe restores your audio device in the background
* Improved: a normalized waveform no longer shrinks while it loads
* Fixed: starting a track or dragging the position could freeze playback for a second or two
* Fixed: some MP3 files with extra data after their tags would not play
* Fixed: an audio device that was off when Vibe opened is used as soon as you turn it on
* Fixed: System Output could stop playback while macOS briefly had no default device
* Fixed: the last few milliseconds of a track could be cut off at high sample rates
* Fixed: undoing a playlist reorder could leave a blank row
* Fixed: the Control Center placeholder art could show the wrong light or dark version
