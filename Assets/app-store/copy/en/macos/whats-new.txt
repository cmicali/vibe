This beta remembers the audio device you chose when it disconnects, and
switches back to it when it returns.

* New: if the output device you chose disappears, because it was switched
  off or unplugged, Vibe falls back to the system output as before but now
  keeps your choice. When the device comes back, Vibe switches to it again
  with Bit-perfect output and Exclusive output as you left them, even after
  a restart. Choosing any other device ends this.
* New: a USB interface plugged into a different port is recognised as the
  same device, and keeps its Bit-perfect and Exclusive settings.
* Fixed: unplugging a device while paused could occasionally show "Audio
  device unavailable" and unload the current track.
* Fixed: with a mouse connected, the Settings window showed scroll bars on
  pages that had nothing to scroll.

A device that returns during playback is switched to at the next pause or
stop, so the track playing is never interrupted.
