* New: Custom CoreAudio playback engine - faster, more stable, much better system event handling
* New: Lock Window Position, in the View menu and Settings > General
* New: Optional volume control (enable in Settings > Audio)
* Changed: r8brain is now the default sample-rate converter (higher quality, less CPU)
* Changed: when another app takes exclusive use of the audio device, Vibe pauses and says so
* Improved: play, pause, and skip no longer wait while Vibe switches audio devices or a device wakes up
* Improved: a stuck network share no longer holds up a different track, or the tags and artwork of cloud files
* Improved: Vibe frees the artwork thumbnails of a playlist you replace
* Fixed: System Output could stop playback while macOS briefly had no default device
* Fixed: the last few milliseconds of a track could be cut off at high up-conversion rates
* Fixed: the Control Center placeholder art could show the wrong light or dark side
* Fixed: undoing a playlist reorder could leave a blank row
