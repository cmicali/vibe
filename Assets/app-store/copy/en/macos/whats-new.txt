This beta replaces Vibe's playback engine. Vibe now reads, mixes and plays
every track itself, straight to your audio device, instead of going through
macOS's general-purpose audio engine. Please report anything that sounds or
behaves differently from 1.13.

* New: a Declick switch in Settings > Audio. On, the default, every start,
  stop, pause, seek and track change ramps the volume over a few milliseconds
  so it cannot click, with Bit-perfect output on too. Off, playback cuts
  instantly and leaves every sample untouched.
* Changed: with Bit-perfect output on, MP3 and AAC files reach your device at
  their full decoded precision, rounded only to your device's own bit depth,
  instead of being rounded to 16 bits first.
* Improved: a file that is slow to read, such as one still downloading, no
  longer holds up play, pause or skip.
* Improved: a reverb or delay still ringing when you pause now rings out in
  full instead of being cut off after 6 seconds.
* Improved: when nothing is playing, Vibe does no background work, and the
  Settings window does none while it is closed.
* Changed: Control Center and the lock screen show your theme's no-artwork
  image for a track without cover art, instead of a blank tile.
* New: Settings > Advanced > Allow bit-perfect on any device, for testing
  Bit-perfect output on Bluetooth and other devices it is normally off for.
* New: Settings > Advanced can list every stage your audio passes through,
  from the file to the device. Click the Version row seven times to show it.
