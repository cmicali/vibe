This beta records more about what happens when a track starts, to track down
a delay some USB speakers show with Exclusive output on.

* Changed: with Bit-perfect output on, Vibe no longer fades or crossfades
  anything. Tracks start, stop, pause and seek instantly, so every sample
  reaches your device unchanged; a cut in the middle of a song may click.
* Changed: with Bit-perfect output on and a 16-bit audio device, MP3 and AAC
  files are now decoded straight to 16-bit, so no conversion step remains
  between the decoder and the device.
* Changed: the time counter now changes the moment the first second has
  played, instead of up to a third of a second later.
* Improved: switching tracks with Bit-perfect output on no longer re-reads
  your device's volume settings each time, which some USB speakers answer
  slowly.
* New: when the app or the audio stalls, the debug info now records exactly
  where, and how long a track's opening silence lasts is recorded even when
  the playlist's equalizer is hidden.
* New: Vibe now records how long after your click each track's audio really
  started, each step of taking over your audio device, when the time counter
  starts moving, and how long a track opens with silence, along with every
  change and notification your audio devices send and any moment the app or
  the audio output stopped responding. Settings > Advanced > Debug info >
  Save… includes it, and saving now works even while playback is stuck.
* New: press M while Vibe is in front to mark a moment in that record, for
  example the moment you hear a problem. It now works with any keyboard
  layout.
* Fixed: some MP3 files with extra data after their tags would not play.
* Fixed: an audio device that is off when Vibe opens is now used as soon as
  you turn it on, and a device plugged into a different USB port keeps the
  bit-perfect and exclusive settings you chose for that port.
* Fixed: losing the last audio output mid-track could skip to the next track
  instead of pausing, and quitting mid-track could move the playlist on.
* Quitting no longer waits on your audio device: the window closes at once and
  Vibe puts the device back the way it found it in the background.
* Fixed: opening Vibe with Exclusive output on could show "Failed to get
  device's audio settings" for bit-perfect output for the whole session.
